                   World Cup 2026 squad lists: England, Scotland, Brazil, USA, Spain, France, Germany, Netherlands, Argentina, Portugal and more
Teams must submit World Cup squads by June 2; Injured players can be replaced from the provisional squad before a team's first match; Mexico host South Africa in June 11 opener; the 104-game tournament ends at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey on Sunday July 19
Thursday 21 May 2026 20:58, UK
                         Image:
                         Sadio Mane got the better of former Liverpool strike partner Mohamed Salah as Senegal beat Egypt
Countries have begun naming their squads for the 2026 World Cup ahead of this summer's tournament.
After   Scotland  revealed their squad on Tuesday , Thomas Tuchel will name his  England  squad on Friday May 22.
The 48 national teams involved are required to register a squad of between 23 and 26 players, including three goalkeepers, by Tuesday June 2.
Only players named in these squads will be eligible to take part, although they can be replaced by a player from the provisional squad in the event of a serious injury or illness, no later than 24 hours before their team's first match at the World Cup.
